Background

Dokyeong Ha — former Visual Creative Director at JYP Entertainment (2017–2025). Directed creative for DAY6 (including members' solo projects), Xdinary Heroes, Yerin Baek, Baek A Yeon, and Bernard Park. In 2017, also worked on marketing for GOT7 and Suzy, and on Stray Kids’ debut campaign. Lectured at SM Universe and ran an in-person K-pop creative class. Now runs D-mension.

D.11 — The Course

K-pop Creative Direction: From First Keyword to Release Day

Hundreds of documents move through a label before one album reaches the world. This course follows that paper trail — start to finish. Eight modules, thirty-two lessons, one workbook. The entire course runs on PRISM, a fictional girl group — every document rebuilt from scratch for teaching, mirroring how the real process is structured.
